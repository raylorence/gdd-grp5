# Level Flow Breakdown
> [!info] Guideline
> Sequence the introduction of environments, spatial hazards, mechanics, and milestone encounters across the progression curve.

| Stage / Zone                          | Visual Theme                                                  | Introduced Mechanic                                                                       | Primary Hazard / Threat                                                 | Key Encounter / Boss                                                                                    |
| ------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Zone 0: The Raid Site**             | Abandoned printing press & dilapidated courtyard              | Basic Movement, Crouching, and Light vs. Shadow detection                                 | Stationary guard sweeps with fixed flashlight cones                     | Narrow exit alley blocked by two guards; tutorial on tossing empty bottles                              |
| **Zone 1: Interior Squatter Alleys**  | Tightly packed shanties, dangling wires, rain puddles         | Dynamic Hiding spots (plastic barrels, crawlspaces) & Breath Suppression                  | Roaming single patrols and creaking floorboards / loose metal sheets    | Checkpoint gate search; player must slip through a laundry line and bypass a locked chainlink fence     |
| **Zone 2: The Wet Market (Palengke)** | Empty stalls, flickering neon signs, butcher tables           | High-Value Environmental Distractions (cutting meat hook ropes, generator short-circuits) | Overlapping patrol sectors and high ambient surface noise               | Central market plaza guarded by a stationary sweep squad; requires coordinating a generator distraction |
| **Zone 3: Estero Drainage Canal**     | Murky floodways, rusted walkways, stagnant sewer culverts     | Single-use Bypass Tools (bolt cutters, maintenance keys)                                  | Fast-moving search patrols with long-range searchlights                 | Rising water levels forcing movement into illuminated sightlines                                        |
| **Zone 4: The Safehouse Perimeter**   | Barricaded street corner, military APC checkpoint, razor wire | Digital Phone Risk (intercepting frequencies while dodging trace scans)                   | Combined high-density static and dynamic patrols; radio tracking sweeps | Final sprint-and-hide gauntlet across the illuminated avenue into the basement threshold                |
