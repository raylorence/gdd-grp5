# Progression & Resources

> [!info] Guideline
> Categorize in-game currencies, acquisition methods, expenditure paths, and character stat progression

- **Primary Mechanic Resource (Distraction Items):** Disposable, single-use environmental pick-ups (glass bottles, cobblestones, tin cans) found in scrap heaps. Used to misdirect guard cones.
- **Secondary Key Resource (Bypass Tools):** Scarce mechanical items (wire cutters, rusted crowbars, padlock keys) scavenged through risky off-path exploration. Expended to unlock route shortcuts and bypass high-risk chokepoints permanently for the run.
- **Information Collectibles (Intel / Dossier Fragments):** Hidden political leaflets, recorded logs, and regime manifests. Collecting these rewards the player with alternative branching narrative endings.

## Economic Inflow/Outflow Matrix

| Resource/Currency                    | Sources                                                                  | Expenditures                                                                     | Cap/Inventory Limit                                            |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Scrap / Glass Bottles**            | Street garbage heaps, abandoned market stalls                            | Thrown to target locations to produce audible distraction ripples                | Max 2 held in inventory                                        |

| **Panic & Stamina Meter**            | Recovers gradually while walking or hiding safely without holding breath | Depleted by continuous sprint or holding breath during close guard inspections   | Visualized via heavy pulse audio and narrowing screen vignette |
