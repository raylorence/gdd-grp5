# World Setting
> [!info] Guideline
> Summarize the environment, visual tone, and primary narrative conflict.

- **Setting:** A high-friction alternate reality set in modern-day Tondo, Manila. In this timeline, extreme sociopolitical instability caused the re-imposition of nationwide Martial Law with contemporary surveillance and militarized police apparatus. Narrow alleys, patchwork galvanized steel roofs (*yero*), tangled electrical cables (*spaghetti wires*), sari-sari stores, and monsoon rain puddles create an oppressive, hyper-dense urban labyrinth.
- **Central Conflict:** Following a violent tactical police raid on an underground pro-democracy resistance council meeting, the protagonist finds themselves stranded deep inside locked-down enemy territory. Carrying encrypted documents exposing human rights violations and regime command structures, the player must outwit curfew patrols and reach the safehouse before total perimeter containment is achieved.
- **Themes:**
  - *Institutional Oppression:* Raw human resilience under overwhelming surveillance and brute military dominance.
  - *Collective Resistance:* Ordinary people sustaining underground solidarity in the margins of society.
# Characters
> [!info] Guideline
> Give every major character a clear motivation (what they want) and a gameplay role (what they do for the player)

- **[[Protagonist]] (The Courier / "Nobody"):**
  - *Background:* An ordinary member of the local resistance movement who was attending their first strategic assembly when the raid occurred.
  - *Motivation:* Surfacing through the cordoned streets of Tondo alive and delivering the courier dossier to the safehouse network.
  - *Gameplay Function:* Controlled avatar; lacks combat potential; relies strictly on spatial navigation, agility, and situational cunning.
- **[[Antagonist]] (Militarized Police & Patrol Officers):**
  - *Motivation:* Enforce curfew, isolate resistance agitators, and silence dissent with absolute authority.
  - *Gameplay Function:* Formidable spatial obstacles equipped with directional flashlights, auditory awareness, search cones, and radio coordination nets.
- **[[Key NPC]] (The Safehouse Keeper / "Kuya Dante"):**
  - *Motivation:* Keep the resistance cell alive while coordinating safe routes through the district.
  - *Gameplay Function:* Communicates through localized encrypted SMS/radio drops at safe points; provides contextual hints, unlockable shortcut tools, and marks transition safehouses.
